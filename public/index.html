About Me

I am a STEM teacher at J.P. Taravella High School. I teach computer science and game design/programming. I am also a lifelong tinkerer, maker and learner. I love putting new things into the world, whether that is through woodworking, art, or coding. Passing on a maker mindset is at the core of my teaching philosophy.
